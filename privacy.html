Digga-Digga privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Digga-Digga is a tunnelling arcade game made by one independent developer. This page explains what the game does with information about you. The short answer: nothing leaves your device.

What the game collects

Nothing. Digga-Digga does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts or sign-ins, no adverts, no analytics or tracking, and no in-app purchases. The Android app doesn't ask for internet access and doesn't connect to the internet.

What is kept on your device

To remember your game between sessions, Digga-Digga saves a few small settings on your own device only:

  • your best scores, the furthest level you've reached and your stars on each level
  • your daily challenge score
  • your choices for difficulty, controls and vibration, and whether you've seen the first-level hints

This information never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. If your phone's own backup is switched on, Android may include it in your device backup, which is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by Digga-Digga.

Permissions

The Android app uses one permission: vibration, so the phone can buzz during play. You can switch this off under Vibration on the title or pause screen.

Children

Digga-Digga collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, for example because a new feature needs the internet, this page will be updated first and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email address shown on Digga-Digga's Google Play page.
